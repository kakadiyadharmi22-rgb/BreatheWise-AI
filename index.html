UNDERSTAND THE NUMBERS

What does your air mean?

◌

Fine particles · PM2.5

Tiny particles that can travel deep into the lungs. Higher readings deserve extra attention, especially for sensitive groups.

◎

Coarse particles · PM10

Includes dust and larger particles. Construction, road dust and some industrial activities can contribute.

⌁

Gases · NO₂, O₃, SO₂

Different gases come from sources such as traffic, sunlight-driven chemical reactions and fuel combustion.
